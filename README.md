## ⚡ Core Features & Capabilities

1. **Royal Preloader Intro Screen:** Animated concentric geometric rings, glowing KS emblem, light sweep, progress counter (`00%`–`100%`), and protocol status updates.
2. **Interactive 3D Physics Tilt:** Dynamic cursor tracking across all Practice Vertical cards, certificates, and estimator cards.
3. **32+ NIC Industry Sectors Showcase:** Dedicated 9th vertical card highlighting Government of India recognized multi-vertical authorization.
4. **Interactive Scope & Ballpark Price Estimator:**
   - Multi-vertical service selector
   - Operational scale toggle (Startup, Established, Enterprise)
   - Dynamic timeline horizon slider (1–12 Weeks)
   - **Dual Currency Switcher:** Instant toggle between `₹ INR` (Domestic) and `$ USD` (Global / NRI clients)
   - One-click WhatsApp dispatch initialization with pre-filled scope summary
5. **Government Statutory Verification Bar:**
   - Single-line horizontal frame with equal 25% column distribution (`GSTIN`, `Udyam No.`, `Direct Line`, `Official Email`)
   - One-click clipboard copy buttons
   - **One-Tap Smartphone Contact Card (`.vcf` vCard download)**
6. **Interactive Certificate Document Modal Viewer:** In-browser inspection of verified statutory specifications for Form GST REG-06, Udyam MSME, and Commercial Premises.
7. **Executive 4-Stage Delivery Framework:** Interactive roadmap mapping Phase I (Discovery) through Phase IV (Turnkey Handover).
8. **Frequently Addressed Inquiries (FAQ):** Accordion answering enterprise questions on IP security, NDA enforcement, and multi-vertical synergies.
9. **Direct Advisory Dispatch / Consultation Hub:** Bespoke custom select dropdown with icon badges and "Others" category, custom luxury validation error states, and direct WhatsApp channel dispatch.
10. **Airtight Legal Governance:**
    - [`terms.html`](terms.html): Master Terms of Service & Legal Engagement Agreement
    - [`privacy.html`](privacy.html): DPDP Act 2023 & IT Act 2000 Data Protection Policy
    - [`refund.html`](refund.html): Master Cancellation, Billing & Non-Refund Policy
11. **Comprehensive Multi-Device Responsiveness:** Fluid scaling across compact mobile phones (320px+), tablets, laptops, and desktop PCs.
12. **PWA & Search Engine Optimization:** PWA Web App Manifest (`manifest.json`), OpenGraph/Twitter rich sharing cards, and Schema.org JSON-LD structured data.
