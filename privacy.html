01 Scope, Corporate Identity & Statutory Applicability

This Data Privacy and Protection Policy ("Policy") outlines the precise terms, protocols, and protective boundaries under which Kothotya Consultancy Services ("KCS", "Firm", "We", "Us"), led by Proprietor Abhishek Sharma, collects, processes, stores, utilizes, and defends business, corporate, statutory, and technical information furnished by clients, website visitors, and corporate stakeholders ("Client", "You").

05 Reasonable Security Practices & Limitation of Cyber Liability

KCS implements industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards (including SSL/TLS transport encryption, access-controlled workstations, and multi-factor authentication for administrative tools) in compliance with Section 43A of the Information Technology Act, 2000.

Limitation of Cyber Liability & Transmission Disclaimers
While KCS maintains rigorous security precautions, no internet transmission or cloud data repository can be certified as 100% impenetrable against state-sponsored intrusions, zero-day vulnerabilities, or upstream telecom breaches. KCS shall not be held liable for any unauthorized access, interception, or corruption of data resulting from vulnerabilities in Client-selected third-party servers, Client credential mismanagement, or unencrypted email transmissions initiated by the Client.

06 Statutory Data Retention & Mandatory Archive Protocols

In strict compliance with statutory commercial statutes of India:

  • Mandatory Tax & GST Audit Records: Transaction ledgers, tax computations, invoice records, and statutory submissions are retained for a minimum period of eight (8) consecutive financial years as mandated by the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 and the Income Tax Act, 1961.
