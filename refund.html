01 Fundamental Principle of Non-Refundability

Kothotya Consultancy Services (KCS) operates exclusively as a premier provider of high-caliber, bespoke professional services, including custom computer programming, web application systems, cloud architecture, statutory GST filings, tax advisory, structural feasibility, and corporate management consulting.

Absolute Non-Refundability of Mobilization Retainers & Dispatched Work
Unlike retail mass-market goods, all engagements undertaken by KCS demand immediate, irreversible allocation of senior specialist hours, executive intellect, mathematical modeling, and cloud development infrastructure from the very moment of commissioning. Therefore, ALL advance booking retainers, mobilization deposits, milestone disbursements, and consultation fees paid to KCS are 100% NON-REFUNDABLE AND NON-CREDITABLE under any circumstances whatsoever once funds are transferred or professional work has commenced.

02 Irreversible Character of Professional Intellectual Services

The Client explicitly understands, covenants, and acknowledges that:

  • Time and Brainpower Cannot Be Reclaimed: Specialist hours expended by KCS consultants, software architects, and statutory tax advisors represent unrecoverable professional services that cannot be returned, re-shelved, or rescinded.
  • Digital Source Code & Advisory Work Product: Once source code, technical blueprints, architecture designs, tax computations, or strategic documents are compiled, shared, or delivered (via staging servers, electronic mail, GitHub repositories, or messaging), they constitute delivered intellectual property and digital goods under the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules and are completely exempt from cancellation or return rights.
  • No "Change of Mind" Refunds: Subjective dissatisfaction, subsequent changes in client internal budgets, corporate reorganizations, personal financial constraints, or shifts in client commercial strategy do NOT entitle the Client to any refund, fee rebate, or discount on commissioned work.

03 Milestone Invoicing & Five (5) Day Deemed Approval Protocol

Projects are executed across defined, progressive operational milestones:

  • Five (5) Day Deemed Acceptance Rule: Upon notification by KCS of the completion of a milestone or delivery of work product, the Client shall have five (5) business days to review the deliverable against the written technical specifications. If no written, specific technical deficiency is communicated in writing to KCS within five (5) business days, the milestone shall be irrevocably deemed fully approved, accepted, and cleared by the Client.
  • Subsequent Revisions: Once a milestone is approved or deemed approved, all associated fees are irrevocably earned by KCS. Any subsequent modifications, rollbacks, or rework shall be treated as a new chargeable change request.

04 Complete Exemption of Third-Party & Statutory Pass-Through Costs

Under no circumstances shall KCS refund or be held liable for any third-party or government disbursements incurred on the Client's behalf, including:

  • Government statutory filing fees, GST/MCA challans, stamp duty payments, and registry taxes paid to sovereign portals.
  • Third-party cloud infrastructure charges (AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, Cloudflare), domain registration costs, SSL certificates, database hosting, or third-party API subscription costs (OpenAI, Twilio, SendGrid, Razorpay).
  • External audit, structural testing laboratory, or specialized legal filing disbursements.

05 Client Cancellation Protocols & Abandonment Liquidation

If the Client seeks to cancel an ongoing engagement prior to final delivery:

Termination Indemnity & Billing for Work Completed
In the event of unilateral termination by the Client, KCS shall retain 100% of all retainers and deposits previously received. Furthermore, the Client shall remain immediately liable to pay KCS for 100% of all hours and work performed up to the exact moment of written notice, plus a twenty-five percent (25%) project interruption and resource rescheduling indemnity fee.

Client Ghosting & Abandonment: If the Client fails to communicate, supply essential inputs, or clear pending invoices for fourteen (14) consecutive calendar days, the contract shall be marked as Abandoned by Client. All advance retainers are irrevocably forfeited, project files are archived, and KCS is fully discharged from all further obligations. Reinstating an abandoned project requires a non-negotiable 35% administrative restoration fee.

06 Strict Prohibition of Unilateral Chargebacks & Payment Disputes

The Client expressly agrees to the following anti-chargeback covenant:

Chargeback Prohibition & Fraudulent Dispute Defense
The Client agrees NOT to initiate, file, or pursue any unilateral chargeback, payment dispute, or claw-back request through any scheduled bank, credit card issuer, UPI provider, or payment gateway (e.g. Razorpay, Stripe, Cashfree, PayPal) without first exhausting formal, good-faith written conciliation with the Proprietor of KCS.
  • Any chargeback or transaction dispute initiated in contravention of this Agreement shall be deemed a material breach of contract and an unlawful commercial tort.
  • The Client agrees to pay KCS, immediately upon demand, the full disputed sum plus an administrative handling fee of INR 25,000 (Twenty-Five Thousand Indian Rupees), along with all legal costs, bank penalties, and statutory compensatory interest at eighteen percent (18%) per annum.
  • KCS reserves the immediate right to file recovery suits and criminal complaints under Section 406/420 of the Indian Penal Code (or Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita) for fraudulent chargebacks.

07 Statutory Scrutiny & Sovereign Authority Disclaimers

KCS delivers expert guidance and statutory compliance advisory. However, the final issuance of tax refunds, Input Tax Credit (ITC) approvals, GST registration approvals, trademark registrations, or judicial assessments depends exclusively upon sovereign Government of India officials and statutory tribunals. No refund or adjustment of KCS professional fees shall be issued on the basis of a sovereign agency's independent statutory assessment, delay, or demand order.

08 Arbitration & Exclusive Jaipur Legal Jurisdiction

Any financial dispute, invoice claim, or controversy arising out of or relating to this Cancellation & Refund Policy shall be governed by the laws of India and resolved exclusively through binding arbitration in Jaipur, Rajasthan under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

Exclusive Legal Seat: Jaipur, Rajasthan
The Client irrevocably submits to the sole and exclusive territorial jurisdiction of the competent courts in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India for all legal actions, recovery proceedings, or enforcement of arbitral awards relating to fees or this Policy.
